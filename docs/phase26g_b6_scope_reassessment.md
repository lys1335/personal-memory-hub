| Empty Chains | 225 proposals |

### 12.3 Assessment

**Is duplication normal?** YES

- Multiple evidence interpretations are expected
- Different proposals may reference the same evidence set
- This represents diversity of interpretation, not error

**Does it affect B.6?** NO

- Duplication exists in proposals, not in candidates
- Candidates are the primary rebuild unit
- Proposals are regenerated during evolution phase

**Should it be handled before Clean Rebuild?** NO

- Clearing all proposals is part of the plan
- New proposals will be generated during evolution
- Duplication rate will be monitored post-rebuild
